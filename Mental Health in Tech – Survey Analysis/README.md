# Mental Health in Tech – Survey Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes mental health trends in the tech industry using a global survey dataset. The goal is to uncover insights related to mental health treatment, the impact of gender, age, remote work, and workplace support in tech companies.

## Dataset
- Source: [OSMI Mental Health in Tech Survey](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/osmi/mental-health-in-tech-survey)
- Size: 1200+ responses from tech employees worldwide
- Format: CSV

## Tools & Libraries
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Analyses
- Gender distribution and mental health treatment rates
- Age demographics of survey participants
- Relationship between mental health treatment and workplace factors
- Visualization of patterns to highlight mental health challenges in tech

## Insights
- Female respondents tend to seek mental health treatment more than male respondents.
- Majority of participants are aged between 25 and 35.
- Remote work shows mixed effects on mental health support.
- Awareness and availability of mental health benefits vary across companies.

## How to Run
1. Clone or download the repository.
2. Place `survey.csv` inside the `data/` folder.
3. Open the notebook `mental_health_analysis.ipynb` in Jupyter.
4. Run all cells to perform the analysis and generate visualizations.
